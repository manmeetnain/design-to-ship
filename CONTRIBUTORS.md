# Contributors and adopters

Design to Ship is improved by people who test the method, report gaps, contribute evidence, build integrations, and share what they learn.

## Maintainer

- [Manmeet Nain](https://github.com/manmeetnain) — creator and lead maintainer

## Contributors

Contributors are credited through Git history, pull requests, and release notes. Significant research, specification, implementation, benchmark, documentation, and community contributions may also be recognized here with the contributor's permission.

## Built with Design to Ship

Projects may be featured when they demonstrate a traceable use of the method and clearly label which parts were verified. Share a project in [Show and tell](https://github.com/manmeetnain/design-to-ship/discussions/categories/show-and-tell) with:

- the product and problem addressed;
- the modes and gates used;
- an example `E-* → R-* → D-* → V-*` chain;
- what was verified and what remains unknown;
- a public link or reproducible artifact when available.

Inclusion is editorial, not certification or endorsement. Maintainers may remove entries that become unavailable, misleading, unsafe, or incompatible with the project values.
