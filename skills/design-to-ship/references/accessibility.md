# Accessibility baseline

Treat accessibility as a design input and verification dimension, not a final overlay.

## Specify

- Semantic roles, names, relationships, and heading order
- Keyboard order, focus movement, visible focus, and escape behavior
- Status announcements and validation messaging
- Contrast and non-color cues
- Zoom, reflow, text resizing, target size, and orientation behavior
- Motion purpose, interruption, and reduced-motion alternatives
- Labels, instructions, errors, and recovery in plain language
- Captions, transcripts, alternative text, and decorative-image treatment

## Verify

Use automated checks to find likely failures, then manually test the critical journey with keyboard navigation and relevant assistive technology when available. Record the environment and scope. Do not report compliance from an automated score alone.

When a normative accessibility claim matters, verify it against the applicable current standard and jurisdiction rather than relying on remembered thresholds.
